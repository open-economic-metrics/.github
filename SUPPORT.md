# Support and questions

Open Economic Metrics is an early-stage open-source research initiative. Community support and response times are not guaranteed.

- **Bugs and reproducibility failures:** Open an issue in the repository that owns the implementation or statistical result.
- **Methodology and sources:** Use a research issue, identifying the source, definition, observation period, and unresolved question.
- **Feature requests and documentation:** Open a focused issue in the affected repository.
- **Contribution process:** Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Security vulnerabilities:** Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than posting confidential details publicly.
- **Community conduct:** Follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Please search existing issues first and avoid including personal, confidential, or licensed-for-limited-use data in public reports.

Public APIs, hosted dashboards, and live support channels will be documented in their respective projects when they actually exist.
